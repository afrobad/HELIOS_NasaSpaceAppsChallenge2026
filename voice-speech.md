## Complete Recording Script (Single Continuous Paragraph)

A heartbeat changes four hundred thousand kilometers from Earth. Who notices? On Artemis missions to the Moon and Mars, twenty-four minutes of radio delay cuts astronauts off from mission control. When microgravity leaches bone density, shifts body fluids to the skull, and swells optic nerves, crew survival depends on one thing: autonomous onboard intelligence. We are Team Aero Vitals—uniting our team lead, developer, system architect, researcher, and video editor to answer NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 with HELIOS: Health Evaluation Logistic Intelligent Onboard System. HELIOS is an offline-first health sentry running directly on spacecraft flight computers without cloud dependence. We grounded our platform in authentic NASA science, fusing SpaceX Inspiration4 datasets from the NASA Open Science Data Repository—OSD-569 for hematology and OSD-575 for multi-omics biomarkers—with live 10 Hz telemetry across 149 parameters. The flight Dashboard gives instant situational awareness: streaming four-crew vitals, hardware-decoupled 90 FPS Lead II ECG waveforms that capture subtle arrhythmia shifts in real time, and cabin ECLSS life-support telemetry monitoring oxygen, carbon dioxide, and air pressure. At its core is the interactive 3D Holographic Bio-Twin. Astronauts rotate a live anatomical twin through 360 degrees, peeling back layers to inspect organ-level strain, bone demineralization, and optic nerve sheath swelling from SANS—turning abstract biomarkers into intuitive spatial insight. Our Astronaut Health Telemetry System organizes measurements across ten physiological categories—linking 119 biomarkers, from cosmic radiation dosimetry to bone resorption and immune cytokines, into actionable operational insight. Because deep-space wellness must be proactive, HELIOS introduces daily Self-Check and dynamic Mission Routine. In Self-Check, crew members log subjective symptoms and pain locations that instantly cross-correlate with biosensors. In Routine, daily schedules dynamically adapt to biometric fatigue: tracking hydration, scheduling targeted ARED resistance exercises to stop microgravity bone loss, and measuring millisecond cognitive alertness with a validated Psychomotor Vigilance Task. When an in-flight crisis strikes, HELIOS acts within forty milliseconds. In our live simulator, trigger a deep-space Solar Radiation Storm: radiation spikes to 85 milligrays per hour, alarms flash crimson, and our offline voice AI, Helix-AI, speaks directly into crew headsets, commanding them into the water-shielded storm shelter. Once shielded, one click on the green SOLVED button harmonically resets systems to nominal flight cruise. Inside our 3D ISS Deck, astronauts can navigate ten station modules to inspect bulkhead sensor telemetry. Health follows the astronaut wherever they go. During lunar and planetary spacewalks, astronauts cannot carry tablets. The hands-free HELIOS Suit HUD projects real-time vitals, suit pressure, oxygen reserves, and tactical waypoint navigation directly onto the helmet visor. Meanwhile, at Earth Mission Control, flight support teams monitor synchronized telemetry through a physics-based speed-of-light delay engine simulating communication latency from low Earth orbit out to Mars conjunction. HELIOS solves the greatest challenge of deep-space human flight, but its architecture extends everywhere on Earth—protecting firefighters in smoke-filled buildings and remote rescue teams operating without network connectivity. Because in deep space, a heartbeat is just data until someone understands it. HELIOS turns raw signals into life-saving action. Monitor. Understand. Act. We are Team Aero Vitals.
