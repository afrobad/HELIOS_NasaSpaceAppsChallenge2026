## [PRESENTATION TIPS] How to Deliver the Winning Speech

1. **The First 15 Seconds:** Do not begin with *"Hello, my name is..."* Start directly with the life-or-death deep-space scenario. Force the judges to lean in and feel the claustrophobic dread of being millions of miles from Earth.
2. **Name the NASA Datasets Verbally:** Judges listen for genuine NASA integration. Emphasize *"NASA Open Science Data Repository, OSD-575 Inspiration4, and NASA-STD-3001"*.
3. **Show, Don't Just Tell:** When you say *"watch what happens during a real crisis"*, have the video demonstrate the emergency banner and the green **"SOLVED"** recovery button simultaneously.
4. **Confident, Decisive Ending:** Finish with the project name (*"This is H.E.L.I.O.S."*), pause, hold eye contact with the camera, and stop talking before the timer hits 4:00.
