> **[2:15 – 2:30]**  
> *"HELIOS features a synchronized dual-domain architecture. On Earth, flight controllers at Mission Control monitor the fleet via the MCC console. But when a spacecraft travels to Mars, physics imposes a twenty-two minute one-way speed-of-light communication lag."*

> **[2:30 – 2:45]**  
> *"By simulating this **Deep Space Network delay**, HELIOS demonstrates why Earth-dependent medical models fail. While Mission Control sees data that is twenty minutes old, HELIOS operates fully autonomously onboard the spacecraft, diagnosing and stabilizing the crew locally."*
