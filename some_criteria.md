1.	Impact: How much good the project can do, including how big the problem is and how many people it could help.
	2.	Creativity: How new the approach is, compared with a small improvement on existing work.
	3.	Validity: Whether the solution is scientifically sound and can work in the real world.
	4.	Relevance: How well the project answers its challenge, how complete and technically feasible it is, and how usable it is.
	5.	Presentation: How clearly the team tells the story of the challenge, the solution and why it matters. The official guide asks teams to give this one equal attention.
